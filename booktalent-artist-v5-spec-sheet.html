About

Piyush Gaur is a luxury wedding anchor and emcee with 15+ years on stage, hosting weddings and events across India, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Moscow, Saint Petersburg, Nepal, Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket and Chiang Mai. He hosts in Hindi, English, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marwari, Sindhi, Marathi and Tamil.

“I believe weddings are meant to be enjoyed without unnecessary time restrictions. If a function scheduled for 10 AM starts in the afternoon, or one planned to end at 2 PM runs till 4 PM, I don't mind. Whether it's the Haldi, Sangeet or Baraat, even if the schedule slips, I'm happy to accommodate — so you and your guests enjoy every moment without feeling rushed.”
